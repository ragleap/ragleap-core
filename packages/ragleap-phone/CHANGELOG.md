# Changelog

All notable changes to ragleap-phone are recorded here, in the Keep a Changelog style.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Package skeleton: `Frame`, a frozen audio frame (PCM 16-bit little-endian, mono) that carries its own sample rate and timestamp, as proposed in docs/design/phase0-adapter-mapping.md. No backends, no calls, nothing released.
- Adapter interfaces from docs/design/phase0-interface.md: `Adapter`, `Call`, `Capabilities` and `Event` (abstract, no backend), and `new_call_id()`. A call id is always made on our side and cannot be passed in. Tested with a fake adapter; nothing is released.
- `Resampler`: a stateful sample-rate converter for `Frame` objects (16 kHz to and from 8 kHz and 48 kHz), standard library only. It carries copies of the ragleap-voice filters, so there is no dependency; a test compares exact output with ragleap-voice when that is installed. Other rate pairs are refused. Nothing is released.
- G.711 audio path: `g711` (mu-law and A-law decode and encode, copied from ragleap-voice with the same caveats: the mu-law encoder and the A-law tables are not verified against the standard) and the stateful `InboundAudio` and `OutboundAudio` (caller bytes to 16 kHz `Frame` objects and back). Standard library only; tests compare with ragleap-voice when it is installed and with audioop where Python still has it. Nothing is released.
- `rtp`: parsing and building of RTP packets for G.711 (version 2, static payload types 0 and 8, extensions refused) and a `Receiver` with the per-packet rules of docs/design/phase0-rtp-endpoint.md (payload type fixed by the operator, one SSRC per call, duplicates and late packets dropped, gaps reported, every outcome counted). Pure functions, no socket, no network. Tested with hand-built bytes, not real captures. Nothing is released.
- `RtpCall` and `EndpointConfig`: the Call half of the RTP media endpoint, without a socket. It checks the source address before parsing, applies the rate limit, the idle and maximum-length timeouts and gap filling with silence, packs outbound audio into 20 ms packets, and keeps only counters. The expected remote address and port are required. `InboundAudio.silence()` feeds lost packets. Tested with a fake clock and hand-built packets. Nothing is released.
- `RtpMediaEndpoint`: the UDP transport for one call. It listens on 127.0.0.1 unless the operator opts in to another address (with a warning that the media is not encrypted or cryptographically authenticated), feeds datagrams to `RtpCall`, sends one packet every 20 ms to the configured remote, pads the last partial packet with silence after about 100 ms of quiet and checks the timeouts once a second. Unit tests use a fake transport; one real-socket loopback test is opt-in (RAGLEAP_PHONE_LOOPBACK=1). Not tested against real PBX traffic. Nothing is released.
